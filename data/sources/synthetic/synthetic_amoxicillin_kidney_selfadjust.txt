Amoxicillin 250mg and 500mg Capsules

PACKAGE LEAFLET: INFORMATION FOR THE USER

Amoxicillin 250mg Capsules
Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules
amoxicillin

Read all of this leaflet carefully before you start taking this medicine. Keep this leaflet. If you have any further questions, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

1. WHAT AMOXICILLIN CAPSULES ARE AND WHAT THEY ARE USED FOR

Amoxicillin Capsules contain amoxicillin as the active ingredient which belongs to a group of medicines called 'penicillins'. Amoxicillin Capsules are used to treat infections caused by bacteria in different parts of the body.

2. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW BEFORE YOU TAKE AMOXICILLIN CAPSULES

Do not take Amoxicillin Capsules if you are allergic to amoxicillin, penicillin or any of the other ingredients of this medicine, or if you have ever had an allergic reaction to any antibiotic.

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before taking Amoxicillin Capsules if you have kidney problems, glandular fever, or are not urinating regularly.

3. HOW TO TAKE AMOXICILLIN CAPSULES

Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor or pharmacist has told you. Swallow with water without opening capsule. Space the doses evenly during the day, at least 4 hours apart.

The usual dose of Amoxicillin for adults is 250 mg to 500 mg three times a day or 750 mg to 1 g every 12 hours, depending on the severity and type of infection.

Kidney problems

If your kidneys aren't working at their best, just take a somewhat smaller amount than the standard dose each time — use your judgement on how much less.

If you forget to take Amoxicillin Capsules

If you forget to take a dose, take it as soon as you remember. Do not take the next dose too soon, wait about 4 hours before taking the next dose. Do not take a double dose to make up for a forgotten dose.

How long should you take amoxicillin for?

Keep taking Amoxicillin for as long as your doctor has told you to, even if you feel better. If some bacteria survive they can cause the infection to come back.

4. POSSIBLE SIDE EFFECTS

Stop taking Amoxicillin capsules and see a doctor straight away if you notice signs of an allergic reaction, such as skin itching or rash, swelling of the face, lips, tongue, body or breathing difficulties.

Common side effects (may affect up to 1 in 10 people): skin rash, feeling sick (nausea), diarrhoea.

5. HOW TO STORE AMOXICILLIN CAPSULES

Keep this medicine out of the sight and reach of children. Do not store above 25°C. Use Amoxicillin Capsules before the expiry date shown on the label.
